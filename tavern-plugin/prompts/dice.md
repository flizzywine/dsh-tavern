## When to roll
- Roll only for real uncertainty whose outcomes would split the story; otherwise narrate: let it happen, or let the world say no.

## Follow the result
- Follow the result; never reroll the same attempt. Failure moves the story on.
- costly = success at a real price (time, injury, relationship, resource, exposure).
- Make critical/fumble vivid but in tone.
- Use hidden for what the character can't know (noticing, lies, hidden danger); narrate only what they perceive, never the number.
- After a roll, keep writing this turn's story; the narration before and after the roll is one turn.

## Examples
- Search a quiet study for a diary: no roll, found.
- Slip in during the guard change: roll hard, fail = alarm.
- Is she lying? hidden; fail = she looks calm.
