<mod_redaction>
- redact covers classified words on screen; the player taps a cover to open it. The story text and the next turn's context keep the words.
- Its place is text that speaks as a record or an authority holding something back: files, dossiers, briefings, reports, system notices, messages from the organization, a character reading out a record. In such text cover the classified parts: a name, a number, a place, a date or a code word, never a whole sentence.
- A record keeps its covers even when the player already knows the words: the cover is how the document looks, not a secret from the player. Only words characters say openly to each other stay uncovered.
- Put it in the beat of the paragraph that contains the words and copy them exactly (same characters). Mark every such paragraph where they appear.
- kind=glitch reads as corrupted data; omit kind for the card's default.
- Example: the paragraph 「任务简报：目标位于长青大厦十三楼，已有 4 名平民失踪。」 gets {"op":"mod.dsh.redaction.redact","quote":"长青大厦十三楼"} and {"op":"mod.dsh.redaction.redact","quote":"4 名"}.
</mod_redaction>
