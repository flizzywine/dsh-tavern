<mod_phone>
- A phone message is sent on a device from one person to a recipient or group: SMS, chat-app messages, calls, voice messages. Mark each such paragraph on kind=line, not narration. Cite its at; keep source text unchanged. Holding a phone alone needs no mark.
- Livestream danmaku and viewer comments, forum or social posts, broadcasts and narration are not phone messages: leave them unmarked in the prose.
- Infer the channel from surrounding narration: kind=line identifies a speaker, not face-to-face speech. A reply following typing/sending is still a text message; mark its own at. Keep the exchange through intervening narration until the story ends it or returns to in-person speech.
- Direct: to is the recipient's handle, or $player for the player. The cited line supplies the sender. Mark both directions of NPC and player exchanges, each with its actual recipient. Text uses form=text; calls/voice messages use form=voice.
- Groups: phone-group defines a stable thread id, name and participants: listed contacts, plus $player when present. Use only source-established members, never the whole contact roster. Use thread instead of to. Reuse a carried group only if its name and members match the source; otherwise create one. Omission keeps groups.
</mod_phone>
